Calibration: Brier Score & ECE
Why Calibration Matters for ERL

The ERL formula is:

ERL = P(attrition) × ReplacementCost

Poorly calibrated probabilities distort ERL, making the economic prioritisation invalid.

Sigmoid calibration reduced ECE by 72% (0.0953 → 0.0264), validating the ERL layer.

Calibration was fitted inside each outer training fold (fold-safe) to prevent leakage.
